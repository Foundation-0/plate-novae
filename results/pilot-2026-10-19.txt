plate-novae pilot: output of pilot/erupt.py, re-run on 19 Oct 2026
==================================================================

Light curves: DASCH DR7, fetched from the DASCH API on 19 Oct 2026 and kept outside this
repository. pilot/README.md gives the API call for each star. Each block below is the unedited
output of the command above it. Running the same command on a fetched light curve gives the
same block.

Columns of each group line: first and last date (decimal years), number of plates (n),
brightest magnitude, plate series.

False alarms
------------

Every group with plates from 2 or more series, on all five stars and in both runs, belongs to a
known eruption or its decline:
  T Pyx 1902, 1920, 1944-45; U Sco 1936 (Run B) and 1945; RS Oph 1933.
  DQ Her 1934.95-1936.90: the eruption of December 1934 and its decline.
  V603 Aql 1918.43 to 1921.36 (four groups): the eruption of June 1918 and its decline. The
  groups fade in turn: brightest 5.82, 6.57, 8.54, 8.96.

Five stars cannot measure a false-alarm rate. Groups that pass only part of the rule:
  Single plates away from any known eruption, Run A: T Pyx 1905.03, 1916.19, 1936.46 (9.27),
  1943.42, 1944.14; U Sco 1900.70, 1909.48; V603 Aql 1915.70 (7.97). T Pyx 1902.77, 1945.94 and
  1947.31 come within three years after an eruption and may be late in its decline.
  Run B adds single plates of T Pyx in 1907, 1926, 1928, 1952 and 1971, and turns T Pyx
  1945.94 into a group of 3 plates from one series (to 1946.18).
  One series only: RS Oph 1914.21-1914.23, 3 plates (below).
