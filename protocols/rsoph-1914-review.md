# RS Oph 1914 review packet

Status: preparation for review. No observational result is claimed.

Status of the historical flag is undecided. The reported March bright points do not establish an eruption. The review interval is 13 February through 25 May 1914 and must include all covering plates, not only detections selected by the pilot.

For every plate record plate ID, series, timestamp and time scale, exposure interval, astrometric solution, detection/limit, quality flags, cutout URL and content hash. Retrieve in small cached batches. Compare same-plate stars chosen by a fixed radius and brightness rule; retain their magnitudes and residuals against their own quiet levels.

A SkyBoT query needs the actual observation time and target position, with an uncertainty radius appropriate to the plate. Save the request and response. A modern asteroid catalogue cannot exclude every unrecorded historical transient; a no-match response is one check, not proof.

The independent reviewer checks image morphology, crowding, plate-edge effects, quality flags, comparison-star offsets and temporal consistency. Record eruption, plate fault or undecided, with reasons and reviewer identity. A person signs the image verdict before any claim or external report. No cutouts, SkyBoT response or signed verdict are fabricated by this packet.
